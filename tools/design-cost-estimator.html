Why prices vary

Concepts: two concepts cost less than five. Revisions: more rounds, more time. Scope: a logo alone is one deliverable; a full identity adds a colour system, typography, stationery, social templates and guidelines. Deadline: rush work costs more. Who designs it: a freelancer’s overhead is lower than a studio’s — but a studio brings a team and a process.

Frequently asked questions

Is this Muhammad Amad’s price?

No. This tool shows indicative 2026 market ranges from published Pakistan pricing guides. Your exact price comes as a written quote after a short brief — send an inquiry through the contact page.

Why do logo prices vary so much in Pakistan?

Scope, concepts, revisions, deadline and who designs it. A freelancer logo with source files is typically PKR 10,000–60,000; an agency logo starts around PKR 40,000–80,000 and a full identity goes past PKR 300,000.

What does a full brand identity include?

Usually a logo, colour system, typography, business card and stationery, social templates and brand guidelines — the full kit a business needs to look consistent everywhere.
