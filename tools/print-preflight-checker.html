Frequently asked questions

What is print bleed?

Bleed is artwork extended past the trim line — usually 3 mm on every side — so the cutter’s small drift never leaves a white edge. Without bleed, a tiny cutting shift shows as an unprinted sliver.

What resolution do I need for print?

300 DPI at the finished size for anything held in the hand — business cards, flyers, labels, packaging. Large-format work viewed from a distance can use less.

Should my print file be CMYK or RGB?

CMYK. RGB is a screen colour mode; printers print in CMYK, and converting at the last minute shifts colours. Design in CMYK from the start for print work.
