About

Design is how an idea becomes real.

I’m Muhammad Amad — a graphic designer and creative lead working on brand identity, packaging and campaigns, and teaching design alongside the work.

I entered design through hands-on practice and formal training, and grew into leadership and teaching roles. My work is practical: a brand has to survive on a real shelf, in a real feed, and in a real brief — not just in a portfolio mockup.

As a teacher, I focus on workflow over shortcuts: how to approach a brand project, how to move from sketch to final design, and how to use AI as part of a designer’s process — not as a replacement for judgement.
